Night Shift

A Sokoban-style puzzle game for the ZX Spectrum 48K. Push every crate onto a target. You can push a crate but never pull it, and only one at a time.

Controls

Q A O P Up, down, left, right
Space Fire: choose a level, pause, confirm

The game starts by itself; press Space on the title screen. Restart from the pause menu if you get stuck: there is no undo.

About

Source code, and the tape file to play in other emulators or on a real Spectrum: github.com/tczajka/night-shift (download nightshift.tap).

The levels are from Microban, a set of Sokoban puzzles by David W. Skinner. The emulator is JSSpeccy 3 by Matt Westcott, under the GNU General Public License v3; its source is in that repository.
